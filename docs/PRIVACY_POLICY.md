# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction

TourneyHub ("Company," "we," "us," "our") operates a youth sports tournament platform ("Platform") that enables athletes, coaches, tournament hosts, and administrators to participate in organized sporting events. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our mobile application, web application, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

**IMPORTANT:** This Privacy Policy is compliant with:
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) - 15 U.S.C. § 6501 et seq.
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - Regulation (EU) 2016/679
- State privacy laws across all 50 U.S. states

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration:**
- Full name
- Date of birth
- Email address
- Phone number
- Physical address
- Username and password

**Profile Information:**
- Athletic position/sport
- Height, weight, and athletic measurements
- School/team affiliation
- Athletic achievements and statistics
- Social media profiles (optional)

**Verification Documents:**
- Government-issued ID (driver's license, passport)
- Birth certificate
- School ID or enrollment verification
- Proof of address

**Payment Information:**
- Payment method type (card, Apple Pay, PayPal, etc.)
- Billing address
- Transaction history
- **NOTE:** We do NOT store complete credit card numbers. Payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant third-party processors (Stripe, PayPal).

**Communication:**
- Messages sent through the Platform
- Support tickets and inquiries
- Feedback and survey responses

### 2.2 Biometric Information (Optional)

If you opt-in to facial recognition check-in:
- Facial biometric template (mathematical representation, NOT image storage)
- Facial recognition attempt logs
- Confidence scores from recognition attempts
- **NOTE:** Raw facial images are NOT stored. Only encrypted biometric templates are retained.

### 2.3 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device type, operating system, and version
- Device identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID)
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Mobile network information

**Usage Information:**
- Pages/features accessed and time spent
- Tournaments participated in
- Check-in records
- Payment transactions
- Clicks, taps, and interactions
- Error logs and crash reports

**Location Information:**
- Approximate location (from IP address)
- Tournament location data
- **NOTE:** We do NOT collect precise GPS location without explicit opt-in.

## 3. Children's Data and COPPA Compliance

### 3.1 Age Restrictions

Our Services are intended for users age 13 and older. However, we recognize that youth athletes under 18 are primary users of our Platform.

### 3.2 Parental Consent for Users Under 18

**For users under 18 years old:**

1. **Parental Account Creation:** A parent or legal guardian must create an account first.
2. **Parental Verification:** Parent identity is verified via email confirmation and optional ID verification.
3. **Child Profile Creation:** Parent creates the child's profile and provides:
   - Child's full name
   - Child's date of birth
   - Parental relationship confirmation
4. **Explicit Parental Consent:** Parent must:
   - Read this Privacy Policy
   - Review the Terms of Service
   - Provide explicit written consent via checkbox
   - Verify email address
   - Accept biometric consent separately (if applicable)

**Consent Storage:**
- Timestamp of consent
- IP address of consenting parent
- Parent email address
- Consent version accepted
- Parental verification method

### 3.3 Special Protections for Users Under 13 (COPPA Compliance)

For users under 13:
- Parental consent is MANDATORY before any account activation
- Parent email verification is REQUIRED
- Child cannot access Services until parental consent is verified
- We do NOT use child data for marketing or targeted advertising
- We do NOT share child data with third parties except as required for tournament operations
- Parent can request deletion of child's data at any time

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Active Account Data

**Retained while account is active:**
- Account information
- Tournament participation records
- Payment history
- Communication records
- Verification documents

### 6.2 Retention After Account Deletion

**Deleted upon user request:**
- Personal information (name, contact info)
- Biometric data (facial templates)
- Verification documents
- **Timeline:** 30 days after deletion request

**Retained for legal/business purposes:**
- Transaction records (7 years - tax/financial requirement)
- Audit logs (3 years - legal defensibility)
- Anonymized usage statistics (indefinite)

### 6.3 Biometric Data Retention

- Retained only while facial recognition is enabled
- Deleted immediately upon opt-out
- Deleted upon account deletion
- **Cannot be recovered once deleted**

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 Right to Access

You have the right to:
- Request a copy of all personal data we hold
- Receive data in a portable, machine-readable format
- **Request process:** Submit request via Settings > Privacy > Request My Data
- **Response time:** 30 days

### 7.2 Right to Correction

You have the right to:
- Correct inaccurate information
- Update outdated information
- **Correction process:** Edit directly in account settings or submit correction request
- **Response time:** 10 days

### 7.3 Right to Deletion ("Right to Be Forgotten")

You have the right to:
- Request deletion of personal data
- Receive confirmation of deletion
- **Exceptions:** Data required by law (tax records, audit logs)
- **Deletion process:** Settings > Privacy > Request Account Deletion
- **Response time:** 30 days

### 7.4 Right to Opt-Out

You have the right to:
- Opt-out of biometric data collection
- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Opt-out of analytics tracking
- **Opt-out process:** Settings > Privacy > Opt-Out Preferences
- **Effective immediately**

### 7.5 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to:
- Receive your data in a standard format (CSV, JSON)
- Transfer data to another service
- **Request process:** Settings > Privacy > Export My Data
- **Response time:** 30 days

### 7.6 Parental Rights (For Users Under 18)

Parents have the right to:
- Access child's data
- Correct child's data
- Delete child's data
- Opt-out of biometric data collection for child
- **Parental request process:** Log in to parent account > Child Settings > Privacy
- **Response time:** 10 days

## 8. Security Measures

### 8.1 Encryption

- **Data in Transit:** All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS 1.2+
- **Data at Rest:** Sensitive data encrypted with AES-256
- **Biometric Data:** Encrypted with AES-256 and stored separately from personal data

### 8.2 Access Control

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Employees access only data necessary for their role
- **Multi-Factor Authentication:** Required for admin accounts
- **Audit Logging:** All access to sensitive data is logged

### 8.3 Monitoring and Detection

- **Intrusion Detection:** Continuous monitoring for unauthorized access
- **Vulnerability Scanning:** Regular security assessments
- **Incident Response:** 24-hour response team for security incidents

### 8.4 Data Minimization

- We collect only data necessary for Services
- We do NOT collect data "just in case"
- We regularly audit and delete unnecessary data
