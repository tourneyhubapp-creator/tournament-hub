# Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** May 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains when refunds are available for tournament entry fees and platform fees paid through TourneyHub. Please read this policy carefully, as refund eligibility is limited and most tournament fees are non-refundable.

---

## 2. Fee Structure and Terminology

### 2.1 Tournament Entry Fee

The fee charged by the tournament host for participation in a specific tournament. This fee is set by the tournament host and varies by tournament, age group, and division.

**Example:** $150 entry fee for a 12U tournament

### 2.2 Platform Fee

The fee charged by TourneyHub for payment processing, platform maintenance, and customer support. Platform fees are calculated as a percentage of the tournament entry fee.

**Example:** 3% platform fee on $150 = $4.50

### 2.3 Total Payment

The sum of tournament entry fee and platform fee.

**Example:** $150 (entry) + $4.50 (platform) = $154.50 total

---

## 3. Refundable Circumstances

Refunds (both entry fee and platform fee) are available in the following circumstances:

### 3.1 Tournament Cancellation

**Eligibility:** Tournament is cancelled by the host before the tournament start date.

**Refund Amount:** 100% of total payment (entry fee + platform fee)

**Timeline:** Refund processed within 7 business days of cancellation

**Example:** Tournament cancelled due to weather. You receive full $154.50 refund.

### 3.2 Tournament Postponement

**Eligibility:** Tournament is postponed to a future date, and you cannot participate in the rescheduled tournament.

**Refund Amount:** 100% of total payment

**Timeline:** Refund processed within 7 business days of postponement

**Requirement:** You must notify us of inability to participate within 7 days of postponement announcement.

### 3.3 Host Error - Disqualification

**Eligibility:** You are disqualified due to an error by the tournament host (e.g., incorrect eligibility determination, administrative mistake).

**Refund Amount:** 100% of entry fee; platform fee may be retained

**Timeline:** Refund processed within 10 business days of error determination

**Requirement:** You must provide evidence of host error and submit appeal within 30 days of disqualification.

### 3.4 Duplicate Charge

**Eligibility:** You are charged twice for the same tournament registration.

**Refund Amount:** 100% of duplicate charge

**Timeline:** Refund processed within 5 business days of duplicate charge confirmation

**Requirement:** You must report duplicate charge within 60 days of transaction.

### 3.5 Unauthorized Transaction

**Eligibility:** Payment was made without your authorization (fraud, stolen payment method, etc.).

**Refund Amount:** 100% of total payment

**Timeline:** Refund processed within 10 business days of fraud investigation

**Requirement:** You must report unauthorized transaction within 60 days of transaction date.

### 3.6 Technical Error

**Eligibility:** Payment was processed due to a technical error on the Platform (e.g., double-submission, system malfunction).

**Refund Amount:** 100% of erroneous payment

**Timeline:** Refund processed within 10 business days of error confirmation

**Requirement:** You must report technical error within 30 days of transaction.

### 3.7 Payment Processor Error

**Eligibility:** Payment was processed incorrectly by the payment processor (Stripe, PayPal, etc.).

**Refund Amount:** 100% of total payment

**Timeline:** Refund processed within 15 business days after payment processor confirms error

**Requirement:** You must report error within 60 days of transaction.

## 4. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds are NOT available in the following circumstances:

### 4.1 Voluntary Withdrawal

**Situation:** You decide not to participate in the tournament after registration.

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Entry fees are paid to secure your spot in the tournament. Once accepted, the tournament host incurs costs (scheduling, staffing, field rental) based on registered participants.

**Exception:** If tournament host offers voluntary withdrawal refund, that policy supersedes this policy.

### 4.2 Player Disqualification for Rule Violation

**Situation:** You are disqualified for violating tournament rules (ineligibility, false information, unsportsmanlike conduct, etc.).

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Disqualification is a penalty for rule violation. You are responsible for ensuring eligibility and compliance with tournament rules.

### 4.3 Player Ineligibility

**Situation:** You do not meet eligibility requirements (age, grade, school, residency, etc.).

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** You are responsible for verifying your eligibility before registration. Ineligibility is your responsibility, not the tournament host's.

### 4.4 No-Show

**Situation:** You register but do not attend the tournament.

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Entry fees are non-refundable for no-shows. Tournament hosts incur costs based on registered participants.

### 4.5 Partial Participation

**Situation:** You participate in some games but not all games in the tournament.

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Entry fees cover tournament participation, regardless of number of games played.

### 4.6 Dissatisfaction with Tournament

**Situation:** You are unhappy with tournament quality, scheduling, facilities, or other aspects.

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Refunds are not available due to dissatisfaction. Feedback should be provided to tournament host for future improvements.

### 4.7 Force Majeure Events

**Situation:** Tournament is cancelled or disrupted due to unforeseen circumstances beyond the host's control (natural disaster, pandemic, war, civil unrest, etc.).

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Force majeure events are not the responsibility of the tournament host or TourneyHub.

**Exception:** If tournament is rescheduled and you can participate, you may transfer your entry to the rescheduled tournament instead of requesting a refund.

### 4.8 Scheduling Conflict

**Situation:** Tournament schedule conflicts with your other commitments.

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** You are responsible for checking the tournament schedule before registration. Scheduling conflicts are your responsibility.

### 4.9 Change of Mind

**Situation:** You change your mind about participating after registration.

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Entry fees are non-refundable once tournament registration is confirmed.

### 4.10 Withdrawal After Tournament Starts

**Situation:** You withdraw after the tournament has begun.

**Refund Status:** Non-refundable

**Reasoning:** Tournament costs are incurred once the tournament begins. No refunds are available for mid-tournament withdrawals.

## 5. Platform Fees

### 5.1 Platform Fee Refund Policy

**Refundable:** Platform fees are refunded ONLY when the entire transaction is refunded (tournament cancellation, duplicate charge, unauthorized transaction, technical error).

**Non-Refundable:** Platform fees are NOT refunded for voluntary withdrawals, player disqualification, or ineligibility.

**Reasoning:** Platform fees cover payment processing, platform maintenance, and customer support, which are incurred regardless of tournament participation.

### 5.2 Platform Fee Calculation

Platform fees are calculated as a percentage of the tournament entry fee:

- Standard rate: 3% of entry fee
- Rate may vary by payment method (e.g., international payments may have higher fees)
- Fees are clearly displayed before payment

## 6. Refund Request Process

### 6.1 Submitting a Refund Request

To request a refund:

1. **Log into your account** on the TourneyHub Platform
2. **Navigate to:** Transactions > Select Tournament > Request Refund
3. **Select refund reason** from the list of refundable circumstances
4. **Provide supporting documentation** (if applicable):
   - For host error: Evidence of error
   - For duplicate charge: Transaction IDs
   - For unauthorized transaction: Fraud report
   - For technical error: Screenshots or error messages
5. **Submit request** and receive confirmation email

### 6.2 Refund Request Timeline

**Submission deadline:** Refund requests must be submitted within 60 days of transaction date

**Processing timeline:**
- Initial review: 5-10 business days
- Determination: 10 business days
- Refund processing: 5-7 business days after approval
- Total timeline: 20-27 business days from submission

### 6.3 Refund Status Tracking

You can track your refund request status:
1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to: Transactions > Refund Requests
3. View status: Pending, Approved, Denied, or Completed

## 7. Refund Processing and Payment Methods

### 7.1 Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed as follows:

**Credit/Debit Card:** Refunded to original card within 5-7 business days

**Apple Pay:** Refunded to associated Apple account within 5-7 business days

**PayPal:** Refunded to PayPal account within 3-5 business days

**Bank Transfer:** Refunded to original bank account within 7-10 business days

**CashApp/Venmo:** Refunded to original account within 3-5 business days

### 7.2 Refund Confirmation

You will receive:
- Email confirmation of refund approval
- Refund amount and reason
- Expected refund date
- Refund tracking number
- Receipt for your records

## 8. Dispute Resolution

### 8.1 Refund Denial

If your refund request is denied, you will receive:
- Email notification with reason for denial
- Explanation of policy and how it applies to your situation
- Instructions for appeal (if applicable)

### 8.2 Appeal Process

To appeal a refund denial:

1. **Review denial reason** carefully
2. **Gather additional evidence** supporting your refund request
3. **Submit appeal** within 30 days of denial:
   - Email: refunds@tourneyhub.com
   - Subject: "Refund Appeal - [Tournament Name] - [Transaction ID]"
   - Include: Original refund request, denial reason, new evidence
4. **Await review:** Appeals are reviewed within 10 business days
5. **Receive determination:** Final decision is provided via email

### 8.3 Chargeback Process

If you dispute a charge with your payment processor (bank, credit card company, PayPal, etc.):

1. **Notify us immediately:** Email disputes@tourneyhub.com with chargeback details
2. **Provide documentation:** Transaction ID, reason for dispute, supporting evidence
3. **Chargeback investigation:** We will respond to payment processor within required timeline
4. **Resolution:** Payment processor makes final determination

**Important:** Chargebacks may result in account suspension or termination if found to be fraudulent.

## 9. Special Circumstances

### 9.1 Tournament Host Refund Policies

Some tournament hosts may offer more generous refund policies than this policy. If a tournament host's refund policy is more favorable, the tournament host's policy applies.

**To check tournament-specific refund policy:**
1. View tournament details on the Platform
2. Look for "Refund Policy" section
3. Contact tournament host if policy is unclear

### 9.2 Refunds for Minors

For refund requests by minors (under 18):
- Parent/guardian must submit the refund request
- Parent/guardian must provide consent
- Refund will be processed to parent's payment method

### 9.3 Refunds for Deceased Account Holders

If an account holder passes away:
- Next of kin may submit refund request with death certificate
- Refund will be processed to estate or designated beneficiary
- Special handling may apply; contact us for assistance
